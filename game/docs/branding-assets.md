# Branding assets

## English

- `ultimate-gold-icon.svg` is the project's custom vector icon: a gold-trimmed Poké Ball with three golden feathers. Its design was approved on 2026-10-22. SHA-256: `5f5d297c714b6e665c4f40d1c61fe3586c92ccbc7ed6ea2d397a8060543d91a5`.
- The wiki and patcher use the same self-contained SVG as their favicon. It contains no scripts, fonts or external references.
- `ultimate-gold-header.png` is the approved AI-assisted Ho-Oh/Johto composition, reused without modifying its pixels or C2PA provenance. SHA-256: `4255034e27af18ec1c65b8809adb66e67279dc4fd46f1d70c5e4fdf82b46afe1`. Its prompt used the [official Ho-Oh Pokédex](https://www.pokemon.com/us/pokedex/ho-oh) and [official artwork](https://www.pokemon.com/static-assets/content-assets/cms2/img/pokedex/full/250.png) as visual references. The web uses localized alternative text; the project name in the image stays unchanged.
- Pokémon imagery and characters belong to their respective rights holders. Ultimate Gold is an unofficial fan project.
