### Feature backlog

- Evolution items: some stones have historical shop locations; verify the target and fill missing purchase coverage.
- Cheat wiki: a full reviewed catalog is planned, including trainer-Pokémon capture.
- HM field use: no learned HM or dedicated Pokémon should be needed after obtaining its HM and badge. This is not implemented in the current target.
- Full story, bosses, physical controller and audio acceptance remain pending.

The former western-town dialogue issue was resolved through normal Pokégear progression. Experimental expansion-engine records, Rookidee and modern-ability prototypes are excluded from this project's 493-species target and its wiki data.
